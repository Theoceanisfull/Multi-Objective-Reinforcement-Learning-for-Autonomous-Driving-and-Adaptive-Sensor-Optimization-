Matched agent replays

Frozen policies from the 1,000-episode run. Same starting scenario; actions produce different traffic interactions. Green car = agent. The road view is simulator truth; the map is observation evidence available to the policy.

Frames are captured at decision boundaries (2 Hz, every 0.5 s), not at the 100 Hz physics rate. “Action” is the command applied during the interval ending at the displayed frame. A finished replay holds its final frame while the others continue.

Map: blue = free-space evidence; red = occupancy evidence; grey = unsupported; yellow cross = ego. Sensor badges: grey = off, amber = warming up, green = ready. Keeping a sensor on does not guarantee a measurement. The map is not a raw camera image or validated ground truth.

This first held-out scenario is illustrative; it does not replace the aggregate evaluation. No external assets or internet connection are needed. Open each GIF for a separate preview, or inspect its CSV for the complete action and reward history.
